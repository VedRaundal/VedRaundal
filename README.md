# 💫 About Me

I'm **Ved J Raundal**, an **Embedded Hardware Design Engineer** based in Pune, India. I design, validate, and bring PCBs, embedded firmware, and electronics to production readiness for robotics, drones, industrial systems, and precision medical devices.

My work covers the full development cycle: schematic capture, component selection, multilayer PCB layout, firmware, hardware bring-up, and functional validation.

- **Hardware:** RF boards, flight controllers, FOC motor controllers, and industrial control electronics.
- **Embedded systems:** STM32 and ESP32, sensor integration, motor control, and communication over CAN, Ethernet, SPI, I²C, and UART.
- **Engineering focus:** Signal integrity, power management, reliable communication, and systematic debugging at the bench.

I'm also exploring **FPGA, Verilog, and analog/VLSI design**, and I'm open to collaborating on embedded hardware and digital design projects.
